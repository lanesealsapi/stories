TG Studios — Calling Cards - Movie Ideas (half of which are parodies)

A simple, scrollable list of concise movie ideas (title — one-line logline).

  1. Sands of Echoes
    A diver finds an old shipwreck whose log reveals a family's hidden past.
  2. Midnight Projection
    A projectionist discovers a reel that plays the city's future crimes.
  3. Perfect Sister
    Two estranged sisters finish their father's unfinished film and uncover old secrets.
  4. The 22nd Letter
    A 22-year-old cryptographer decodes a child's book that exposes a conspiracy.
  5. Bowling Night
    A small-town bowling league doubles as a front for high-stakes gambling and redemption.
  6. Lizard on the Lane
    A tiny lizard brings small miracles to a down-on-his-luck bowler during a tournament.
  7. Tinytune
    An indie label fights a corporate takeover while launching an unexpected star.
  8. Aqua-Woman: Storm's Eye
    A weather presenter falls for a mermaid and must choose between worlds.
  9. The Magnifier
    A scientist builds a giant magnifying glass and witnesses something society tried to hide.
  10. Gold in the Ground
    A prospector's map leads a town to fortune—and moral collapse.
  11. Glass House
    A woman's smart home turns invasive, forcing her to fight for privacy and agency.
  12. Late for the Storm
    Meteorologists race to warn a town while their own lives unravel on live TV.
  13. The Subpoena
    A paralegal improvises a plan to subpoena evidence that could free an innocent client.
  14. Revengers
    A misfit team right small injustices in big, hilarious ways.
  15. Aqua Notes
    A musician records underwater soundscapes and hears a voice calling her name.
  16. The Last Reel
    Archivists race to save a film that contains a dangerous truth about a national hero.
  17. Smallest Magnifier
    A child prodigy invents a tiny lens that changes how people perceive truth.
  18. Nocturne for Two
    A pianist and a burglar form an unlikely bond through thin apartment walls.
  19. The Gratitude App
    An app meant to help sobriety becomes the backbone of a healing community.
  20. City of Mirrors
    A blackout forces a city to confront its past and the reflections it has ignored.
  21. Under the Lighthouse
    A keeper's journal reveals a shipwrecked child's claim to a hidden inheritance.
  22. Paper Skies
    A paper airplane competition leads a shy teen to an unexpected journey across town.
  23. Echo Park
    Neighbors unite to save their park and end up uncovering community-wide corruption.
  24. Midnight Bakery
    A late-night baker's pastries become the secret meeting point for lovers and liars.
  25. Signal Lost
    When all signals vanish, two strangers must navigate a city without modern maps.
  26. The Archivist's Daughter
    An archivist's child discovers a tape that reopens a decades-old mystery.
  27. Paper Gold
    A town runs a fake gold rush to save itself, but the lie grows teeth.
  28. Night Shift Choir
    Hospital night staff form a choir that heals patients—and themselves.
  29. The Quiet Thief
    A cat-burglar who steals only sentimentals begins to question the targets she's chosen.
  30. Paper Trail
    A journalist follows a string of receipts to a money laundering ring with a human face.
  31. Last Light Cinema
    A dying cinema screens people's memories instead of films, changing lives nightly.
  32. Glass Harbor
    A coastal town built on recycled glass holds the key to a vanished ship's mystery.
  33. The Weather Man's Daughter
    A famous weatherman's child inherits the power to change local storms—at a cost.
  34. Neon Saints
    An unlikely group of street preachers become accidental influencers, complicating faith and fame.
  35. The Memory Market
    In a future where memories are traded, a seller loses the one memory that defines them.
  36. Salt & Signal
    A coastal radio operator broadcasts lost messages that reunite people decades later.
  37. The Borrowed Name
    A man assumes a dead friend's identity and discovers a life he'd never imagined.
  38. Paper Lanterns
    A festival of lanterns reveals family histories when each light carries a secret confession.
  39. Stray Frequency
    A ham radio hobbyist picks up a signal that shouldn't exist, and follows it cross-country.
  40. The Last Lighthouse Keeper
    The last keeper stays to keep a promise, but the sea keeps asking for repayment.
  41. The Silent Orchestra
    A deaf conductor forms an orchestra that plays to vibrations and changes a city's rhythm.
  42. Out of Season
    A seasonal worker extends her stay and uncovers the town's hidden winters.
  43. The Greenroom
    Backstage rivalries turn into alliances when a canceled show forces performers to improvise life together.
  44. Paper Atlas
    A lost atlas maps not places but people's regrets, and a traveler uses it to make amends.
  45. Home Movie
    Old home footage reveals a neighbor's forgotten heroism, sparking a town-wide rediscovery.
  46. The Locksmith
    A locksmith starts opening doors he was never meant to—and finds the lives behind them.
  47. Paper Wings
    A paper plane revolution starts with a child's wish and grows into a community movement.
  48. Blue Harbor Heist
    A crew plans a heist under the light of a lighthouse to steal back what's been taken from fishermen.
  49. The Composer's Map
    A composer follows a map of sounds to recreate a lost symphony that heals a divided town.
  50. Under Glass
    An urban gardener grows a forbidden plant that turns a neighborhood upside down.
  51. The Debt of Days
    A man agrees to trade years of his life to fix his mistakes and learns what time truly costs.
  52. The Watchmaker's Secret
    A watchmaker repairs more than time—he fixes people's broken moments with mechanical miracles.
  53. Paper Boats
    Children send paper boats down a river; years later, a letter returns to redraw their futures.
  54. Harbor Lights
    Lights in the harbor begin spelling messages at night, and one woman deciphers a call for help.
  55. The Fifth Reel
    A missing reel from a cult classic holds the director's hidden confession.
